## 0. Before Start
You need
1. Github account (Free!)
2. A computer
3. Network(Maybe VPN is necessary)
4. Obsisian(Optional, but I think it's the best note editor)

Have a look to my blog: [Welcome to Sisyphus Mountain](https://sisyphus-mountain.github.io/)
